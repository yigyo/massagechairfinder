## Pain Point Breakdown

**1. Lower back pain** (most frequently cited, multiple discussions)
Context: Mentioned by buyers researching recovery, heat therapy, and SL-track coverage. Often described as chronic and linked to desk work. Some buyers also have coexisting conditions such as sciatica or disc issues.

**2. Neck and upper back tension** (second most frequent)
Context: Strongly associated with long hours of computer work and sitting. Buyers are explicitly seeking chairs that focus intensity on the neck, trapezius, and the area between the shoulder blades.

**3. General tension and stress after long work days** (third)
Context: Less clinical than the above, but drives daily use behavior. Buyers describe needing to decompress rather than treat a specific injury.

**4. Sciatica pain** (fourth)
Context: Raised as both a purchase consideration and a safety question. Buyers want to know whether a chair will help or aggravate the condition.

**5. Head and neck tension** (fifth)
Context: Appears in ASMR-adjacent content and in direct product questions. Buyers seeking targeted neck relief often feel that standard massage chairs underdeliver on this area.
