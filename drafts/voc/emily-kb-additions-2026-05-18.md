### Finding a massage chair for a tall person with broad shoulders on a budget
**What buyers say:** "Looking for massage chair recommendations for a 6'2" 220 lb man with broad shoulders, budget is under $2k"
**Emily's response guidance:** Tall buyers with broad shoulders face two specific fit challenges: shoulder width coverage and track length. Emily should acknowledge these are real constraints at the under-$2k price point and help the buyer understand exactly what to look for rather than just listing models.
**Key points to cover:**
- L-track chairs generally serve taller users better than S-track because the rail extends further down the lumbar and into the glutes
- Shoulder airbag width varies by model and is often not listed clearly in specs, so Emily should encourage buyers to check listed max shoulder width or ask directly
- At under $2k, options exist but the field narrows, so fit should be prioritized over feature count
- Broad-shouldered buyers sometimes find the shoulder airbags feel pinching rather than relaxing if the chair is too narrow
- Suggest /finder to filter by user height and body type before committing to a model

### What is the hip and glute rolling feature called on zero gravity chairs?
**What buyers say:** "What is the proper term for a full hip and glute rolling feature on zero gravity massage chairs?"
**Emily's response guidance:** This is a terminology question that often signals a buyer doing serious research, and getting the vocabulary right helps them search and compare more effectively. Emily should give a clear, confident answer.
**Key points to cover:**
- The feature is most commonly called "glute kneading," "hip and glute massage," or "buttock massage" depending on the brand
- On L-track chairs, the roller naturally travels into the glute area as part of the extended track design, which is different from dedicated airbag compression of the hips
- Some chairs combine roller-based glute coverage (via L-track) with airbag side compression on the hips, and these are distinct mechanisms worth understanding separately
- Buyers researching this feature specifically should look for L-track roller coverage depth in the spec sheet, and confirm whether the hip airbags squeeze laterally or if the rollers actually knead the glute muscle

### Calf and foot massage quality in massage chairs
**What buyers say:** "What's the best calf and foot massager and massage chair?"
**Emily's response guidance:** Emily should recognize that this buyer may be prioritizing lower-body relief specifically, which meaningfully narrows the field and changes what features matter most. Lower-leg massage quality varies more across chairs than most buyers expect.
**Key points to cover:**
- Calf and foot massage on massage chairs is delivered primarily through airbag compression, with some models adding rollers under the foot platform for plantar stimulation
- Buyers who want serious foot relief should specifically look for chairs with foot roller features, not just airbag calf wraps, as the experience is substantially different
- The depth and wrap of the calf airbags matter; wider, multi-chamber designs tend to cover more of the leg and feel more thorough
- Some buyers with specific lower-body needs (plantar fasciitis, poor circulation, leg fatigue from standing work) find that a dedicated foot and calf massager paired with a full chair gives more targeted relief than a chair alone
- Suggest /finder if the buyer wants to filter by lower-body massage quality specifically
