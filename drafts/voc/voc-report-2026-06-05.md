## Pain Point Breakdown

1. **Back pain (general)** -- The single most mentioned complaint, appearing across multiple platforms and discussion types. Buyers reference it as both a purchase motivator and a measure of product success.

2. **Lower back tension and lower back pain** -- Mentioned specifically and frequently, often tied to desk-work posture and long daily sitting hours. Buyers want to know whether heat coverage reaches the full lumbar area.

3. **Neck pain and upper back pain** -- Commonly paired together, often linked to prolonged screen time and poor workstation posture. One buyer described their neck and upper back as "absolutely cooked."

4. **Sciatica pain** -- Mentioned as a concern around chair suitability, with buyers uncertain whether massage or heat could aggravate rather than help.

5. **Shoulder tension and mid-back tightness** -- Expressed in visceral terms, with one buyer noting their "shoulders are locked." This pain point drives interest in chairs with strong upper-body airbag and roller coverage.
