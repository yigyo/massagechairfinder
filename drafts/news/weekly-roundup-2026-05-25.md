## Technology and Innovation

**Does Zero Gravity Actually Work? Medical Breakthrough Weighs In**

[Medical Breakthrough](/brands/medical-breakthrough) published a blog post this week examining whether [zero-gravity positioning](/learn/zero-gravity) delivers real therapeutic benefit or is primarily a marketing feature. The post comes from a brand with a stake in the answer, so readers should weigh it accordingly. That said, the underlying question is legitimate and comes up frequently in the MCF inbox. The short answer, supported by published research on spinal decompression and pressure distribution, is that zero-gravity recline does reduce compressive load on lumbar discs and can meaningfully improve how deeply a roller system engages the back musculature. The benefit is real, but it is not transformative on its own. What zero gravity does is create a better starting position for the massage, not replace it. Buyers with chronic lower back pain or disc issues should treat zero-gravity support as a necessary feature rather than a premium add-on when comparing chairs in the $3,000-and-up range.

A separate Medical Breakthrough piece this week told the story of a buyer whose neck pain worsened after purchasing a budget chair. The story reinforces a point MCF makes consistently: chairs below the $2,000 threshold often lack the shoulder width, neck roller range of motion, and [body-fit adjustability](/learn/body-fit) that taller or broader buyers need. A chair that cannot properly position its rollers relative to your cervical spine will not help neck pain and can make it worse.

## Brand News

**Luraco Earns Medicare Approval, DME Licensing, and FDA Registration**

This is the most substantive brand development of the week. [Luraco](/brands/luraco) announced that it has simultaneously achieved Medicare approval, Durable Medical Equipment (DME) licensure, and FDA registration for its massage chair technology. No other brand in the MCF review range holds this combination of designations.

What this means for buyers: Medicare approval opens a pathway for qualifying patients to seek partial or full reimbursement for a Luraco chair as prescribed durable medical equipment. This is not guaranteed reimbursement for every buyer, and it requires working through a physician and a Medicare-enrolled DME supplier. But the pathway now exists, which is a material change for buyers who have chronic pain conditions, are Medicare-eligible, and have been told by a physician that massage therapy is part of their treatment plan.

FDA registration means Luraco's devices are listed in the FDA's device registration database, a step that validates manufacturing and quality control processes. DME licensing governs the legal framework for billing medical equipment to insurers. Together these three designations position Luraco as the only consumer massage chair brand with a credible medical reimbursement story. Buyers researching Luraco's current lineup should verify the specific models covered under these designations directly with the brand, as coverage may not extend to every chair in the portfolio.

**Massage Magazine: Top 10 Chairs Ranked by 1,400+ Licensed Massage Therapists**

This is the most interesting third-party review piece of the week. [Massage Magazine](https://www.massagemag.com/top-massage-chairs-278650/) surveyed more than 1,400 licensed massage therapists across private practices, spas, and mobile settings to identify the top-performing chairs in actual treatment environments. Professional therapists evaluate chairs differently than consumers do. They prioritize adjustability across a wide range of client body types, durability under heavy-use conditions, and the quality of roller contact at specific muscle groups. Consumer reviews tend to weight first-impression comfort and ease of use more heavily. The gap between those two sets of priorities is instructive for buyers who want a chair that will hold up and deliver consistent therapeutic value over years of use, not just feel impressive in the first session. Worth reading for anyone who is serious about the therapeutic side of the purchase.
