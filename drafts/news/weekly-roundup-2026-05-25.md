The biggest story in the massage chair category this week has nothing to do with a new model. [Luraco](/brands/luraco), the Arlington, Texas manufacturer that holds the distinction of being the only American-made medical massage chair brand, announced it has achieved simultaneous Medicare approval, Durable Medical Equipment (DME) licensure, and FDA registration. That triple designation, if it holds up in practice for buyers, could change how health insurance intersects with high-end massage chair purchases in a meaningful way.

## Brand News

**Luraco Earns Medicare Approval, DME License, and FDA Registration**

Luraco announced on May 6, 2026, that it has cleared three regulatory hurdles at once: Medicare approval, DME licensure, and FDA registration. The press release positions this as a "landmark advancement for the future of therapeutic wellness," though the practical question for most buyers is a simpler one. Can I use insurance or Medicare to offset the cost of a Luraco chair?

The short answer, based on what the brand has announced, is that the pathway now exists. DME licensure means Luraco chairs can be classified as durable medical equipment by qualifying providers, which opens the door to Medicare reimbursement under certain clinical circumstances. FDA registration speaks to the brand's positioning as a medical device manufacturer rather than a consumer electronics company. No other brand in the Massage Chair Finder (MCF) review range holds this combination of designations simultaneously.

That said, buyers should approach this with realistic expectations. Medicare coverage for durable medical equipment is condition-specific and requires a prescribing physician. The designation does not mean that any buyer who wants a Luraco chair can file a Medicare claim. MCF will follow up with more detailed guidance on how to navigate the reimbursement process once we have spoken directly with Luraco about which models qualify and what documentation buyers need. For now, if you are purchasing a chair for a diagnosed medical condition and your physician is open to prescribing DME, Luraco is the only brand currently positioned to work within that framework.

Buyers considering [Luraco's current lineup](/brands/luraco) should ask their dealer directly about DME documentation at the point of sale.

**Infinity Circadian DualFlex Named IBT Editor's Pick**

[Infinity](/brands/infinity) received the International Business Times 2026 Editor's Pick designation for the Circadian DualFlex. Award coverage like this is useful for buyers as a confidence signal, though MCF evaluates chairs on body-fit, track performance, and long-term value rather than editorial accolades alone. The Circadian DualFlex uses a dual-roller system designed to address both the upper and lower spine in a single session. We will be taking a closer look at the verified specs as more owner data comes in. For buyers currently researching Infinity's lineup, the IBT recognition is worth noting alongside the chair's feature set.

**Ceragem on Home Healthcare Positioning**

[Ceragem](/brands/ceragem) received some trade press attention this week around its "decoupling" strategy, a positioning approach that frames the brand's products as bringing clinical-grade healthcare into the home rather than selling consumer wellness products. An MSN piece cited marketing commentary praising this framing. For MCF readers, the practical takeaway is that Ceragem continues to position itself at the intersection of wellness and clinical use, which is relevant context when comparing it to Luraco's now-formalized medical device status. The strategies are philosophically similar but the regulatory backing is currently different.

**Nouhaus at High Point Market 2026**

[Nouhaus](/brands/nouhaus) used High Point Market 2026, held in April, as the venue to unveil what it is calling "restorative massage technology" aimed at countering the effects of sedentary work and screen-heavy lifestyles. The brand framed the launch around the broader health consequences of prolonged sitting. Specific model names and verified specs from the High Point showcase have not been confirmed by MCF as of this writing. We will publish a dedicated post once we have confirmed product details. Buyers interested in Nouhaus should check back in the coming weeks.

## Worth Reading

**Consumer Reports: 3 Best Chair Massagers of 2026**

Consumer Reports published its tested roundup of chair massagers for 2026. The Consumer Reports methodology emphasizes lab-measured performance and user comfort panels, which overlaps with but is not identical to MCF's evaluation framework. MCF focuses on the $2,000 to $10,000-plus range with an emphasis on [track type](/learn/track-types), [roller dimensions](/learn/roller-dimensions), and long-term [body fit](/learn/body-fit). **Massage Magazine: Top 10 Chairs Ranked by 1,400+ Licensed Therapists**

Massage Magazine's survey of more than 1,400 licensed massage therapists, published in 2025, is one of the more credible practitioner-facing rankings in the category. Therapists evaluated chairs in working clinical settings, not as consumers, which makes their criteria different from a home-buyer perspective. They care about session consistency, client positioning, and build durability under daily use. That said, a chair that performs well in a professional treatment room for years is a meaningful quality signal for a home buyer who wants to make a long-term investment. The [full list is at Massage Magazine](https://www.massagemag.com/top-massage-chairs-278650/) and is worth bookmarking alongside your primary research.

**Garage Gym Reviews: Relaxe Chair Tested**

Garage Gym Reviews published a 2026 hands-on review of a chair from [Relaxe](/brands/relaxe). The publication's audience is performance and recovery-focused, so their evaluation lens emphasizes muscle recovery and session intensity rather than relaxation or therapeutic positioning. That framing may actually be a useful complement to standard massage chair reviews for buyers who are purchasing primarily for post-workout recovery. MCF has not independently verified the Relaxe specs discussed in the review, so treat the numbers as a starting point.
