The biggest story this week has nothing to do with a new chair. [Luraco](/brands/luraco), the only U.S.-based manufacturer in the premium massage chair space, announced it has simultaneously achieved Medicare approval, Durable Medical Equipment (DME) licensure, and FDA registration, a combination that no other brand in the Massage Chair Finder (MCF) review range holds. That news landed alongside two meaningful product launches: [Kyota](/brands/kyota) introduced the Konbi M728 DualPro 4D, and [Human Touch](/brands/human-touch) opened sales on the Super Novo 3.0. It was a busy week for buyers who are actively researching their next chair.

## New Chairs and Launches

**Kyota Konbi M728 DualPro 4D**

Kyota announced the Konbi M728 DualPro 4D this week, positioning it as a luxury-feature chair at value pricing. The "DualPro 4D" name suggests a [4D roller system](/learn/roller-dimensions) as the centerpiece of the massage experience, meaning roller speed and intensity can be modulated in real time rather than following a fixed program. Kyota has not released detailed specifications through verified channels beyond the press announcement, so we are treating specs as preliminary until the chair ships and we can confirm figures independently. Based on the brand's existing lineup, a price point somewhere in the $4,000 to $6,000 range would be consistent with Kyota's positioning, but no pricing has been officially confirmed. We will update our coverage once the chair is available for hands-on evaluation. Buyers comparing this to existing chairs in that range should keep an eye on [track type](/learn/track-types), which the current announcement does not clarify.

**Human Touch Super Novo 3.0**

Human Touch announced that the Super Novo 3.0 is now available for purchase, placing it within the brand's premium Ascent Series. This is a successor to the Super Novo line, which has been one of the more recognizable names in the upper tier of the category. The press release describes the chair as expanding the Ascent Series, but specific updates over the previous Super Novo generation have not been detailed in verified materials. Buyers researching the [Human Touch lineup](/brands/human-touch) should confirm current pricing and available colorways directly, as Ascent Series chairs have historically shipped in limited finishes. We will add the Super Novo 3.0 to the MCF catalog once we have verified specification data.

## Technology and Innovation

**Ceragem and Intellivix: AI Wellness Spaces Partnership**

[Ceragem](/brands/ceragem) signed a memorandum of understanding with Intellivix, an AI imaging and analytics firm, to develop what they are calling AI-based smart wellness spaces. The practical question for buyers is whether this partnership will affect any chair currently shipping or available for purchase, and the honest answer is: not yet. MOUs of this type typically describe R&D intent rather than production-ready features. What it does signal is that Ceragem is investing in sensor-driven personalization, the idea that a chair could assess your body and adjust its program accordingly without manual input. That outcome matters to buyers who find current body-scan features too imprecise. We will follow this one if it moves from agreement to product.

## Brand News

**Luraco: Medicare Approval, DME License, and FDA Registration**

This is the story of the week for anyone considering a massage chair for therapeutic rather than purely recreational use. Luraco announced it has achieved all three designations simultaneously: Medicare approval, Durable Medical Equipment licensure, and FDA registration. What this means for buyers is significant. Medicare approval opens a potential reimbursement path for qualifying patients, which could meaningfully reduce out-of-pocket cost for buyers purchasing on medical grounds. DME licensure means the product meets regulatory standards for medically prescribed equipment. FDA registration places Luraco's chairs within a formal oversight framework. No other brand in the MCF review range holds this combination of designations. Buyers whose physicians have recommended massage therapy as part of a treatment plan should ask their provider whether a Luraco chair qualifies under their specific plan. Pricing for Luraco chairs remains at the premium end of the category, but the insurance angle changes the cost conversation.

**Infinity Circadian DualFlex Wins IBT Editor's Pick**

[Infinity](/brands/infinity) received the International Business Times 2026 Editor's Pick award for the Circadian DualFlex. Third-party awards from mainstream media outlets are worth noting because they reflect how chairs perform for general buyers, not just enthusiasts. The Circadian DualFlex has drawn attention for its dual-flex track design, which is intended to follow the spine's curvature more closely than a standard [L-track or S-track](/learn/track-types). The IBT recognition adds credibility to that positioning. We will cross-reference this against MCF's own evaluation criteria when we publish a full review.
