The biggest story this week was not a new chair launch. [Luraco](/brands/luraco) became the first massage chair brand to simultaneously hold Medicare approval, DME licensure, and FDA registration, a combination that meaningfully separates it from every other brand in the premium category. Elsewhere, the [Infinity](/brands/infinity) Circadian DualFlex picked up a prominent third-party award, Consumer Reports and Fortune both published 2026 buyer guides worth bookmarking, and Massage Magazine released a large-scale professional survey that tells a different story than most consumer roundups.

## New Chairs and Launches

There were no confirmed chair launches this week. [Nouhaus](/brands/nouhaus) showcased what it describes as "restorative massage technology" at High Point Market 2026 in late April, framing it around the sedentary health crisis that desk workers and remote employees know well. The press materials are thin on specifics: no model names, no track type, no pricing, and no published specs as of this writing. High Point Market is primarily a furniture trade show, so it is worth monitoring whether what Nouhaus demonstrated is a chair or a technology platform that will appear inside a future chair. We will follow up once the product has a name and verifiable details.

## Technology and Innovation

No discrete technology announcements came through this week, but two brand stories have technology implications worth flagging.

Luraco's new certifications carry direct consequences for what its chairs can do in a clinical setting. Medicare approval means a physician can prescribe a qualifying Luraco chair and a patient may be reimbursed through Medicare, a first in the category. DME (durable medical equipment) licensure means Luraco chairs meet the federal standards applied to therapeutic devices, not just consumer electronics. FDA registration means the manufacturing process and product claims have been reviewed under federal oversight. Taken together, these are not marketing designations. They are compliance milestones that reflect how the product is built and what it is permitted to claim. For buyers who need a chair that a physician or insurer will take seriously, no other brand in the Massage Chair Finder (MCF) review range holds this combination of designations. Full details on what these certifications cover are in the Luraco brand announcement linked in the Brand News section below.

[Bodyfriend](/brands/bodyfriend) also signaled a broader technology direction this week, describing an expansion of its product ecosystem into what it calls "comprehensive wellness solutions." The coverage is light on specifics, referencing healthcare devices and digital health integration without naming particular products or features. This aligns with a broader industry trend toward chairs that connect to health monitoring systems, but buyers should treat this as a brand direction signal rather than a purchasable feature set for now.

A third-party award from a general-interest business publication is not a clinical certification, but it does reflect the chair's crossover appeal to buyers who are not deep in the category. Buyers researching [Infinity's current lineup](/brands/infinity) can use the Chair Finder to see how the Circadian DualFlex fits their body profile and space.

**Ceragem signs wellness housing MOU with HDC I-PARK.** [Ceragem](/brands/ceragem) has signed a memorandum of understanding with Korean developer HDC I-PARK to build wellness-focused residential housing in Seoul. Two separate outlets covered this story, which signals that Ceragem is pursuing an integration strategy well beyond standalone product sales. For buyers in North America considering a Ceragem chair for home use today, this partnership has no near-term product impact. It does suggest the brand is investing in the idea that wellness hardware belongs inside the built environment rather than as an afterthought purchase. MCF will watch whether this strategy produces any North American-market developments.

**Bodyfriend expands into healthcare ecosystem.** Bodyfriend's announcement this week pointed toward integrated health device platforms without naming specific chairs or launch dates.
