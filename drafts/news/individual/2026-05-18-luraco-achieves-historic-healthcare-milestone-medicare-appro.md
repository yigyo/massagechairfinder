Luraco Health & Beauty LLC announced today that it has achieved a combination of three significant healthcare credentials: Medicare approval, Durable Medical Equipment (DME) licensing, and FDA registration. According to the company, Luraco is the only American manufacturer of medical massage chairs to hold all three designations simultaneously, a claim that, if accurate, would represent a meaningful separation from competitors in the therapeutic segment of the market.

## What Was Announced

According to Luraco, the company has secured the following:

- **Medicare approval**, meaning Luraco chairs may now be eligible for Medicare reimbursement in qualifying clinical or patient contexts
- **DME (Durable Medical Equipment) licensure**, a designation issued to equipment suppliers and manufacturers that meet federal standards for medically necessary devices
- **FDA registration**, which indicates that Luraco's manufacturing facility and relevant products are registered with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration

Luraco describes this milestone as advancing "medical massage technology into mainstream medicine." The company has long marketed itself as the only massage chair manufacturer based in the United States, producing chairs in Arlington, Texas, and has positioned its products at the intersection of consumer wellness and clinical therapy. These new credentials appear to formalize that positioning within the U.S. healthcare system.

## What This Means for Buyers

For most consumers shopping for a home massage chair, Medicare approval and DME licensure are not factors they encounter. But for a specific and growing group of buyers, this announcement is genuinely significant.

**Buyers with qualifying medical conditions** may now have a pathway to partial or full reimbursement for a Luraco chair through Medicare, depending on how their physician documents the medical necessity and which chair model is involved. This is not a guarantee of coverage, and buyers should consult their healthcare provider and Medicare plan before assuming reimbursement applies. That said, no other brand in the [massage chair price range MCF covers](/about) has previously achieved this combination of designations.

**Clinical buyers, physical therapy practices, and rehabilitation facilities** have long used massage chairs as adjunct therapy tools, but procurement through Medicare-reimbursable channels has been complicated. Luraco's DME licensing opens a more formal purchasing path for those institutional buyers.

**General consumers** benefit indirectly. FDA registration and DME licensure require manufacturers to meet documentation, quality control, and safety standards that go beyond what typical consumer electronics or wellness products must satisfy. A chair built to those standards represents a different tier of manufacturing accountability than an imported consumer product with no equivalent oversight.

For buyers already considering Luraco for therapeutic reasons, such as recovery from surgery, chronic back pain, or musculoskeletal conditions, these credentials add a layer of third-party validation that press releases and star ratings cannot replicate. Buyers researching [Luraco's full model lineup](/brands/luraco) will find chairs priced in the $5,000 to $9,000 range, which is consistent with the investment level that tends to attract buyers with genuine medical or long-term therapeutic needs rather than casual wellness interest.

## What We Do Not Know Yet

Several important details are not yet clear from the announcement:

- **Which specific Luraco models** are covered under the Medicare approval or DME designation. It is possible that only certain chairs qualify, rather than the entire product line.
- **The reimbursement process and dollar amounts.** Medicare coverage for durable medical equipment involves prior authorization, physician documentation, and specific billing codes. Luraco has not yet published consumer-facing guidance on how a buyer would initiate a claim.
- **Whether this affects pricing or availability.** DME-classified products sometimes carry different pricing structures than their consumer retail equivalents, and it is not yet known whether Luraco will offer distinct SKUs or configurations for the medical channel.
- **Exactly which FDA registration category applies.** FDA registration of a manufacturing facility is different from FDA clearance or approval of a specific device. The announcement does not specify which registration classification Luraco has received.

MCF will update this article as Luraco releases additional documentation.

## How It Compares

No other brand in the [MCF catalog](/chairs) currently holds all three of these healthcare credentials, which makes a direct apples-to-apples comparison difficult. That said, it is worth placing Luraco's positioning in context relative to other high-end therapeutic chairs.

The [Luraco i9 Max](/chairs/luraco-i9-max) competes most directly with chairs like the [Infinity Circadian](/chairs/infinity-circadian) and upper-tier Osaki models in the $7,000 to $9,000 range. Those chairs offer competitive feature sets, including 4D rollers, body scanning, and zero-gravity recline, but they are manufactured overseas and carry no equivalent medical-grade credentials. For a buyer whose primary motivation is therapeutic benefit and who may be working with a physician or insurer, Luraco's certification stack now creates a distinction that feature lists alone cannot address.

For buyers whose interest is primarily in massage quality and value, the certifications do not change the comparative roller performance, airbag coverage, or body fit of any chair. Those buyers should still evaluate chairs on [track type](/learn/roller-tracks), recline range, and fit for their body dimensions, using the [Chair Finder](/finder) as a starting point.

The healthcare credentials matter most to a specific type of buyer. For that buyer, they matter quite a lot.

Luraco's announcement represents the most significant regulatory milestone any massage chair brand has achieved in the U.S. market to date. Whether it translates into meaningful Medicare reimbursement for individual buyers will depend on details the company has not yet published, but the underlying designations are real and carry weight in ways that marketing language typically does not.
